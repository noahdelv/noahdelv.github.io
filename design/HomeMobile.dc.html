[ ABOUT ]
I got into computer science through games, and game development remains a central part of how I think about software. Building a co-op underwater exploration game from scratch taught me more about systems design than most coursework: how input latency shapes feel, how UI communicates state under pressure, how a single mechanic can define or sink an experience.
Currently I work as an AI Data Specialist at Handshake, designing adversarial prompts to stress-test multimodal LLMs and building computer vision training datasets. I'm looking for roles in software engineering or game development.
